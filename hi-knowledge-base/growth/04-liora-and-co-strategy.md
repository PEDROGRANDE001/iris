# Leveraging Liora and Co for Higher Margins & Direct Sales

*Updated: March 18, 2026*

## Strategic Role of Liora and Co

Liora and Co should serve as HI's **upstream design arm** — capturing projects at the design stage (higher margins, more control) rather than competing on procurement alone (commodity, margin pressure).

## Margin Architecture

### Current State (Procurement-Only)
```
Developer → Architect → Interior Designer → FF&E Specifier → HI (procurement) → Manufacturer
                                                                    ^
                                                           HI captures 15-25% margin here
```

### Target State (Design + Procurement)
```
Developer → Liora and Co (design) → HI (procurement) → Manufacturer
                ^                          ^
        30-45% margin              15-25% margin
        (design fees)              (procurement markup)
```

**Combined margin: 45-70% on projects where Liora leads design**

## Revenue Opportunities for Liora and Co

### 1. Hospitality Interior Design Services
- Full-service interior design for hotels, resorts, branded residences
- Concept through FF&E specification
- Fee structure: 8-15% of construction cost or per-square-foot
- **Key advantage:** Liora specifies → HI procures. Closed loop.

### 2. Model Unit / Sales Gallery Design
- Branded residence developers need stunning model units to sell $1M+ condos
- High-margin, high-visibility work
- Leads directly to bulk FF&E orders for the full building
- South FL pipeline: Waldorf Astoria, Baccarat, Villa Miami, Pagani — all need model units

### 3. Custom Furniture Lines
- Design proprietary furniture collections under the Liora brand
- Sell direct to:
  - Hotels (guest rooms, lobbies, restaurants)
  - Branded residences (owner packages)
  - High-end residential clients
  - Multifamily developers (amenity spaces)
- **Margin advantage:** Manufacturing direct (via HI's supplier relationships) vs. third-party brands = 50-70% gross margin

### 4. E-Commerce / Direct-to-Designer Sales
- Launch a Liora and Co online platform for:
  - Curated hospitality-grade furniture collections
  - Trade program for designers and architects
  - "Owner's packages" for branded residence buyers
- Low overhead, high margin, builds brand awareness

### 5. Wellness/Spa Furniture Collection
- Design specialized spa and wellness FF&E:
  - Treatment beds and tables
  - Meditation pods
  - Recovery lounge furniture
  - Sauna/steam room benches
  - Biophilic design elements
- Capitalize on the wellness trend (fastest-growing hotel segment)
- Sell to resort spas, standalone wellness centers, luxury gyms

## Positioning Strategy

### Brand Differentiation
- **Liora and Co** = Design, taste, curation, luxury aesthetic
- **Higney International** = Procurement muscle, logistics, global sourcing, delivery
- Together: "From concept to installation under one roof"

### Target Client Segments for Liora
1. **Boutique hotel developers** (10-100 rooms) who want unique design, not cookie-cutter brand standards
2. **Branded residence developers** who need residential warmth with hotel-grade durability
3. **Restaurant/bar groups** opening multiple locations (recurring design work)
4. **Wellness resort developers** seeking differentiated spa experiences
5. **Luxury multifamily developers** for amenity space design

### Pricing Strategy
| Service | Fee Structure | Typical Project Size |
|---------|--------------|---------------------|
| Full hotel interior design | 8-15% of construction cost | $500K-$5M+ |
| Model unit design | $150-$300/sq ft | $75K-$300K |
| Amenity space design | $100-$200/sq ft | $50K-$500K |
| Restaurant/bar design | $100-$250/sq ft | $50K-$250K |
| Design retainer | $2K-$10K/month | $24K-$120K/year |
| Custom furniture line | Cost + 60-70% markup | Varies |

## Competitive Advantages to Exploit

1. **Vertical integration** — Design + procurement + logistics in one company (rare in the market)
2. **South Florida location** — Proximity to luxury development epicenter + Caribbean logistics
3. **Hospitality DNA** — Unlike pure residential designers, Liora understands durability, brand standards, and operational requirements
4. **Speed** — Controlling both design and procurement eliminates the handoff delays between separate firms
5. **Cost control** — One P&L means ability to bundle pricing attractively while maintaining overall margin
