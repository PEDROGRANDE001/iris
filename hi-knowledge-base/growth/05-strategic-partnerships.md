# Strategic Partnerships — Architects, Developers, Operators

*Updated: March 18, 2026*

## Partnership Strategy Overview

HI's growth depends on being specified into projects early. The earlier HI/Liora is involved, the higher the margins and the harder it is to be displaced. Partnerships are the mechanism.

## Tier 1: Architecture & Design Firms

### Why Partner
- Architects specify FF&E vendors or recommend them to developers
- Getting on an architect's preferred vendor list = recurring deal flow
- Liora and Co can co-bid with architecture firms on hospitality projects

### Target Firms (South FL Focus)
| Firm | Specialty | Why Target |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Arquitectonica** | Miami-based, luxury hotels/condos worldwide | Designed multiple South FL towers; massive pipeline |
| **Nichols Brosch Wurst Wolfe (NBWW)** | Hospitality, mixed-use, South FL | Deep hotel portfolio |
| **Kobi Karp Architecture** | Miami luxury high-rise, hospitality | Active in branded residence market |
| **ADD Inc / Stantec** | Mixed-use, hospitality | Convention center hotels |
| **Perkins&Will** | Hospitality, wellness, workplace | Growing wellness/resort practice |
| **HKS Architects** | Major hospitality practice | Designed hotels globally |
| **Gensler** | Hospitality interiors, workplace | Could partner on amenity space design |

### Partnership Models
- **Preferred vendor agreements** — Firm recommends HI/Liora for FF&E procurement on their projects
- **Co-marketing** — Joint presentations to developers, shared case studies
- **Design collaboration** — Liora handles interior FF&E design while architecture firm does shell/core
- **Referral fees** — 2-5% referral commission for introductions that convert

## Tier 2: Developers & Owners

### Why Partner
- Developers who trust HI will use them across multiple projects
- Master service agreements = predictable multi-year revenue
- Owner relationships survive management company changes

### Target Developers (South FL + Caribbean)
| Developer | Active Projects | Opportunity |
|-----------|----------------|-------------|
| **Related Group** | Waldorf Astoria Miami, Baccarat Residences, multiple towers | Largest luxury developer in South FL |
| **Terra Group** | Grand Hyatt Miami Beach, multiple projects | Convention hotel + mixed-use |
| **Swire Properties** | Mandarin Oriental Brickell Key, Brickell City Centre | Long-term luxury developer |
| **Tavistock Development** | Pier Sixty-Six / Omni Fort Lauderdale | $1B+ marina redevelopment |
| **Turnberry Associates** | Aventura luxury developments | Long-standing South FL developer |
| **Fortune International** | Multiple condo-hotel projects | High-volume developer |
| **Gencom** | Ritz-Carlton, luxury resort operator/developer | Caribbean + LatAm footprint |
| **Playa Hotels & Resorts** | All-inclusive Caribbean resorts | Hyatt, Hilton brand management |

### Partnership Structures
- **Master Service Agreements (MSAs)** — Preferred FF&E vendor across developer's portfolio
- **Equity participation** — On select projects, invest in FF&E package for equity upside (advanced)
- **Early involvement agreements** — HI/Liora engaged at schematic design phase, not after construction
- **Volume pricing commitments** — Discounted rates in exchange for guaranteed multi-project flow

## Tier 3: Hotel Management Companies & Operators

### Why Partner
- Management companies influence FF&E decisions for owners
- One relationship with a management company = access to entire portfolio
- Brand PIPs (Property Improvement Plans) create predictable FF&E demand

### Target Operators
| Operator | Portfolio Size | Opportunity |
|----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Marriott International** | 94 deals signed in CALA (2025), 45 conversions | Conversions = immediate FF&E. Largest CALA pipeline |
| **Hilton** | 150+ pipeline projects in CALA, 300+ operating | Luxury expansion (Waldorf, Conrad, LXR) |
| **Aimbridge Hospitality** | Largest US third-party manager | Handles renovations for thousands of properties |
| **Highgate** | Major owner/operator, active in South FL | Direct procurement influence |
| **Driftwood Capital** | South FL-based, develops + operates | Built Westin Cocoa Beach, active pipeline |
| **Apple Hospitality REIT** | 220+ hotels | Ongoing renovation cycle |
| **Playa Management** | All-inclusive Caribbean | Hyatt/Hilton branded resorts |

### Partnership Models
- **Approved vendor list** — Get on Marriott/Hilton/Hyatt approved vendor programs
- **PIP partnership** — Position as go-to FF&E partner for property improvement plans
- **Bulk purchasing agreements** — Management companies with 10+ properties get volume pricing
- **Turnkey renovation packages** — Design (Liora) + procurement (HI) + installation as a single vendor
