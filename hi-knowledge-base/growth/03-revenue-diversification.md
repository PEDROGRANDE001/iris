# Revenue Diversification — Recurring Revenue & Predictable Income

*Updated: March 18, 2026*

## The Problem with Project-Based Revenue

HI's current model is likely project-based: win a hotel FF&E contract, deliver, move on. This creates:
- Revenue volatility between projects
- Feast-or-famine cash flow
- No ongoing client relationships after delivery
- Zero switching costs (client can use different vendor next time)

## Recurring Revenue Opportunities

### 1. FF&E Maintenance & Lifecycle Programs

**Concept:** Subscription-based FF&E maintenance contracts with hotels post-installation.

**What's Included:**
- Scheduled inspections (bi-monthly for luxury, monthly for upscale)
- Rotation programs (furniture rotation every 6-8 weeks for luxury properties)
- Deep cleaning coordination (every 6 months)
- Replacement forecasting dashboards (12-18 month lookahead)
- Emergency procurement (avoiding 20-35% rush premiums)
- Audit-ready compliance logs

**Financial Impact for Clients:**
- Extends mattress lifespan from 5 years to 7-8 years
- Cuts 10-year replacement costs by 30-50%
- Example: 100-room property saves $160K-$320K over 10 years on mattresses alone
- Reduces room downtime from FF&E issues

**Revenue Model for HI:**
- Monthly retainer per property: $2,000-$10,000/month depending on property size
- 100 properties = $2.4M-$12M annual recurring revenue
- Margins: 40-60% (mostly labor + coordination, low COGS)

**Implementation:**
- Start with existing clients — offer as post-project value-add
- Use digital tools: asset tracking software, automated scheduling, photo-documented inspections
- Tier pricing by hotel class (luxury/upscale/midscale)

### 2. Design Retainer Programs (via Liora and Co)

**Concept:** Annual design retainer for hotel management companies and developers.

**Services Under Retainer:**
- Seasonal refresh consultations (lobby, F&B, pool areas)
- Model unit/show unit design for new phases
- Brand standards compliance reviews
- Mood boards and material palettes for renovations
- Vendor pre-qualification and specification writing
- On-call design support (X hours/month)

**Revenue Model:**
- Annual retainers: $24K-$120K/year per client
- Includes guaranteed first-look on FF&E procurement for any projects
- Creates lock-in: client relies on Liora for design → HI for procurement

---

### 3. Warehousing & Logistics Services

**Concept:** Offer FF&E warehousing, staging, and logistics as a standalone service.

**Why It Works:**
- Hotels need somewhere to store FF&E during phased renovations
- Receiving, inspection, assembly, staging, installation, debris removal
- HI likely already has warehouse infrastructure in Hollywood FL

**Revenue Model:**
- Storage fees: per pallet/month
- Handling fees: per item received/shipped
- Installation services: per room/per project
- White-glove delivery and placement

---

### 4. Sustainability & Asset Recirculation Program

**Concept:** When hotels renovate, take back old FF&E for resale/recycling/donation.

**Revenue Streams:**
- Removal/hauling fees
- Resale of gently-used hospitality furniture (secondary market is growing)
- Tax-deductible donation coordination (hotels get write-off, HI gets fee)
- ESG/sustainability reporting for hotel clients

**Why Now:** Hotels are under increasing pressure for ESG compliance and sustainability reporting. A circular FF&E program checks both boxes.

---

### 5. Refresh/Renovation Cycle Contracts

**Concept:** Lock in multi-year agreements tied to brand-mandated renovation cycles.

**Hotel Brand Renovation Cycles:**
| Brand Tier | Typical PIP Cycle | FF&E Refresh |
|-----------|-------------------|--------------|
| Luxury | 5-7 years | Soft goods 3-5 years |
| Upper Upscale | 6-8 years | Soft goods 4-6 years |
| Upscale | 7-10 years | Soft goods 5-7 years |

**Structure:**
- Sign 10-year master service agreement
- Guaranteed vendor for all FF&E needs through the cycle
- Phased delivery (floors at a time to minimize disruption)
- Predictable annual revenue from each contracted property
