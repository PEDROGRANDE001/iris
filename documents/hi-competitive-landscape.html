HI's Competitive Moat
What no competitor has:

Peter Higney has 25 years of operating luxury hospitality venues — 12 beach clubs, hotels, restaurants across 4 continents. He designed the Nikki Beach brand. Most FF&E competitors are designers or purchasing agents. None of them have run the venues their furniture goes into.

The result: HI designs for operational reality, not just aesthetics. We know what breaks, what guests actually touch, what looks great in a rendering but fails in 90°F salt air. That gap between design intent and operational durability is HI's core value proposition.
