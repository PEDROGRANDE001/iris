# Stemsmiths.ai — One-Pager

**Date:** March 13, 2026
**Team:** Doug Feirstein, Peter Higney, Austin Higney

---

## The Problem

AI music tools like Suno let anyone create songs in minutes. But the output isn't authentic — bad note selection, over-compression, no human feel. The tracks sound *almost* right, which is worse than sounding wrong. The uncanny valley of music.

Meanwhile, millions of talented musicians — guitarists, trumpet players, saxophonists, drummers, bassists — aren't making money from their skills. There's no efficient marketplace connecting their talent to the people who need it most.

## The Solution

**Stemsmiths.ai** is a two-sided marketplace that bridges AI music creation and human musicianship.

**For Music Creators:**
- Upload an AI-generated track (from Suno, Udio, etc.)
- The platform breaks it into stems (vocals, guitar, drums, bass, keys, etc.)
- Flag which stems need the human touch
- Stemsmiths submit competing recordings for each stem
- Creator listens, picks their favorites, reassembles the track
- Result: AI speed + human authenticity

**For Musicians (Stemsmiths):**
- Browse open stem requests matching your instrument
- Record and submit your take on your own schedule
- Compete on quality, not speed
- Get paid when your stem is selected
- Finally monetize your talent through a dedicated channel

## Why This Works

1. **No turnaround pressure.** Unlike on-demand session work, stemsmiths record at their own pace. Creators aren't expecting instant delivery — they're browsing options.
2. **Competition drives quality.** Multiple stemsmiths submit takes per stem. The best performance wins. This is a meritocracy.
3. **AI does the heavy lifting.** The 80% (composition, arrangement, structure) is handled by AI. Humans close the last 20% where it actually matters — feel, nuance, authenticity.
4. **Scalable unit economics.** No per-user AI agent costs. The platform facilitates connections and takes a cut. Musicians set their own rates.

## Who It's Not For

- People who need a finished song in 2 hours (the original concept — killed for good reason)
- Social media creators needing fast background music (AI-only tools serve them fine)
- Professional studios with session musician budgets (they already have their people)

## The Market

- **Suno** has 12M+ users generating songs daily
- The creator economy is massive and growing
- Working musicians are underemployed — gig economy for music doesn't really exist yet
- No current platform specifically connects AI music creators to human session musicians

## Revenue Model

- Platform commission on each stem transaction
- Premium tiers for creators (priority placement, faster matching)
- Premium tiers for stemsmiths (verified profiles, portfolio features)
- Potential: subscription model for high-volume creators

## What We Shelved
