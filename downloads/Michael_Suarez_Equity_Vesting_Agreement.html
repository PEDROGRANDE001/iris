Equity Incentive
Vesting Agreement

This Equity Incentive Vesting Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of __________________, 2026 ("Effective Date"), by and between:

Higney International LLC, a Florida limited liability company, with its principal place of business at 1720 Harrison St, Suite 16A, Hollywood, FL 33020 ("Company"),

and

Michael Suarez, an individual ("Recipient").

Company and Recipient are each a "Party" and collectively the "Parties."

Recitals

WHEREAS, Recipient provides valuable services to Company as an Independent Sales Representative pursuant to a separate Independent Contractor Sales Representative Agreement dated __________________, 2026 (the "IC Agreement");

WHEREAS, Company desires to incentivize Recipient's long-term commitment and contribution to Company's growth by granting Recipient the opportunity to earn an equity interest in Company;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises herein and other good and valuable consideration, the Parties agree as follows:

1. Equity Grant

1.1 Grant. Company hereby grants Recipient the right to earn up to four percent (4%) of the membership interests in Higney International LLC (the "Equity Interest"), subject to the vesting schedule and conditions set forth herein.

1.2 Nature of Interest. The Equity Interest shall be a profits interest (as defined under IRS Revenue Procedures 93-27 and 2001-43) unless otherwise specified by Company's tax advisor. Recipient acknowledges that this grant is intended to qualify as a profits interest and that no capital interest is being conveyed at the time of grant.

1.3 No Purchase Price. No purchase price is required for the Equity Interest. The consideration is Recipient's continued service and contribution to Company.

2. Vesting Schedule

2.1 Vesting Period. The Equity Interest shall vest over a period of five (5) years from the Effective Date according to the following schedule:

Anniversary Incremental Vesting Cumulative Vested
Year 10%0%
Year 21.0%1.0%

Year 41.0%3.0%

2.2 Cliff. No portion of the Equity Interest shall vest until the first anniversary of the Effective Date. If Recipient's service terminates for any reason before the first anniversary, Recipient shall have no right to any Equity Interest.

2.3 Continuous Service Required. Vesting is contingent upon Recipient's continuous service to Company under the IC Agreement (or any successor agreement). Any interruption or termination of service shall immediately cease further vesting.

2.4 Acceleration. In the event of a Change of Control (as defined in Section 6), all unvested Equity Interest shall immediately vest in full, provided Recipient is in active service at the time of such event.

3. Forfeiture and Repurchase

3.1 Forfeiture of Unvested Interest. Upon termination of Recipient's service for any reason, all unvested portions of the Equity Interest shall be immediately and automatically forfeited.

3.2 Repurchase Right — Voluntary Departure. If Recipient voluntarily terminates service or is terminated for Cause (as defined below), Company shall have the right (but not the obligation) to repurchase all vested Equity Interest at fair market value as determined by an independent valuation or by mutual agreement. Company must exercise this right within ninety (90) days of the termination date.

3.3 Repurchase Right — Termination Without Cause. If Company terminates Recipient's service without Cause, Company shall have the right to repurchase vested Equity Interest at fair market value, payable in equal installments over twelve (12) months.

3.4 Cause Defined. "Cause" means: (a) material breach of the IC Agreement or this Agreement; (b) fraud, dishonesty, or willful misconduct; (c) conviction of a felony or crime of moral turpitude; or (d) failure to meet the Minimum Performance Threshold as defined in the IC Agreement, if applicable.

4. Rights and Restrictions

4.1 Distributions. Once vested, Recipient shall be entitled to distributions proportional to Recipient's vested Equity Interest, at such times and in such amounts as determined by Company's Managing Member(s).

4.2 No Transfer. Recipient may not sell, assign, transfer, pledge, or otherwise dispose of any Equity Interest (vested or unvested) without Company's prior written consent.

4.3 No Management Rights. This Equity Interest does not confer any management, voting, or decision-making authority. Company shall continue to be managed by its existing Managing Member(s).

4.4 Operating Agreement. Recipient agrees to execute Company's Operating Agreement (or an amendment thereto) as a condition of receiving any vested Equity Interest, and to be bound by all terms thereof.

5. Tax Matters

5.1 Section 83(b) Election. Recipient may, in consultation with Recipient's own tax advisor, file an election under Section 83(b) of the Internal Revenue Code within thirty (30) days of the Effective Date. Company makes no recommendation regarding such election.

5.2 Tax Responsibility. Recipient is solely responsible for all tax consequences arising from the grant, vesting, and disposition of the Equity Interest.

5.3 Tax Distributions. Company shall use reasonable efforts to distribute to Recipient amounts sufficient to cover Recipient's tax obligations arising from Recipient's allocated share of Company income, consistent with distributions made to other members.

6. Change of Control

6.1 Definition. "Change of Control" means: (a) the sale of all or substantially all of Company's assets; (b) a merger or consolidation in which Company is not the surviving entity; or (c) the transfer of more than fifty percent (50%) of Company's membership interests to a third party or group of third parties in a single transaction or series of related transactions.

7. General Provisions

7.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

7.2 Entire Agreement. This Agreement, together with the IC Agreement and the Operating Agreement, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding the subject matter hereof.

7.3 Amendments. This Agreement may only be amended in writing signed by both Parties.

7.4 Severability. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

7.5 Notices. All notices shall be in writing, delivered by email, certified mail, or hand delivery.

7.6 Independent Legal Advice. Each Party acknowledges the opportunity to seek independent legal counsel before executing this Agreement.
