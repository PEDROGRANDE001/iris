## The Honest Take

Peter, July is the month that exposes the one real weakness in this business: it can only do one thing at a time, and that thing is you. When you point yourself at Ritz procurement, the execution is flawless — 78 task updates, POs flying, a Dec delivery on track. But new business doesn't slow down in that mode, it stops. The Ritz POs mean the money is real even though the scorecard says $0. But don't let a purchasing-heavy month become an excuse to stop selling for four weeks. The pipeline is a fire; in July you stopped feeding it.
