## Executive Summary

March was a **reality check** month. After February's infrastructure surge (HubSpot, Iris, Michael Sin, systems everywhere), March tested whether those systems would translate into execution. The answer: **partially**.

The "To Respond" email queue grew again.

February built the car. March showed Peter is still sometimes driving it in circles.
