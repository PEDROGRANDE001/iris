## 🔴 Where Peter Needs to Improve

### 1. Task Completion Discipline — D
**This is the biggest issue.** Peter creates tasks and sets deadlines but repeatedly misses them. By the end of January, multiple items were already building up:
- Material selections promised but not delivered
- Spec submissions that slip week after week
- Items like "Email Michael Hawkins re: Lush Life" that sat untouched for weeks

**The pattern:** Peter is excellent at identifying what needs to happen (the task creation is thorough) but weak at blocking time to actually execute the small/medium tasks. The big meetings happen, the site visits happen, the travel happens — but the desk work accumulates.

**Recommendation:** Block 2 hours every Tuesday and Thursday morning as "Task Execution" time. No meetings, no calls. Just knock out the spec submissions, material selections, and email follow-ups. These aren't glamorous, but they're what keeps projects moving between the big moments.

### 2. Email Response Time — C+
Multiple inbound emails tagged "To respond" from January remain unanswered into March:
- Vanessa Cardaci (Knallhart) — Wood Coffee Table order
- Adriana Caicedo (Iberia Tiles) — CORALINA SAMANA
- Daniel Dixon (Akula) — office visit follow-up
- Alicia Cannon — ALIS VIP party RSVP
- Tarell Greene (DDC) — furniture delivery
- Eva Vessio (Blondie's) — Ritz Carlton revised proposal

Some of these are vendor/partner relationships that need nurturing. Leaving emails in "To respond" for 5+ weeks damages credibility.

**Recommendation:** Adopt a 48-hour rule — if an email needs a response, it gets one within 48 hours even if it's a placeholder ("Got this, reviewing, will get back to you by [date]"). Have Iris or Shameen flag anything over 72 hours.

### 3. Overcommitting on Calendar — C
The calendar is packed with micro-tasks scheduled as events (send this email, pay this bill, look into this). Many of these aren't true calendar events — they're to-dos. This clutter makes it hard to distinguish actual commitments from aspirational tasks.

**Recommendation:** Use Monday.com for tasks, calendar for meetings/appointments only. Stop scheduling "Send Bill info about the fireplace" as a calendar event at 6:30 AM.

### 4. Scope Creep on Personal Tasks — B-
During a busy business month, Peter was also:
- Apartment touring (The Tropic)
- Estate planning with multiple lawyers
- Motorcycle parts ordering (WalzWerk)
- Insurance and financial admin
- Driver's license tickets

None of these are wrong — they're life. But they compete for the same limited energy. The personal admin tends to get calendar slots while business follow-ups don't.

**Recommendation:** Batch personal admin to one half-day per week (Saturday afternoon or Sunday). Keep weekday bandwidth for HI.

### 5. Pipeline Tracking — C+
The Active Proposals board got 2 new entries in January (Metro Lagoons, Hyatt Place). But there's no systematic pipeline review cadence. Deals like Chatwal, Four Seasons, and others exist in email threads and memory but aren't consistently tracked with stages, values, and next actions.

**Recommendation:** The HubSpot migration (decided in Feb) should fix this. But it needs to actually happen and be used weekly.
